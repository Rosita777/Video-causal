\section{Task Scope and Seven-Mechanism Ontology}
\label{app:task-ontology}

\outlineblock{Reviewer question}{
What operationally counts as a source, trigger, receiver, and footprint, and
why are these seven mechanisms the frozen scope?}

\subsection{Causal-Role Notation}
\label{app:causal-role-notation}

\outlineblock{Content}{
Provide the complete event notation and its mapping to prompts, target worlds,
atomic review fields, and case identifiers. Include one compact causal graph
showing source to trigger to footprint on the receiver.}

\subsection{Seven Mechanism Definitions}
\label{app:mechanism-definitions}

\outlineblock{Table A1}{
For every mechanism, list source role, trigger, receiver, downstream
footprint, counterfactual state, and acceptable alternative cause. Preserve
the registered order used by every macro result.}

\subsection{Source and Receiver Ontology}
\label{app:source-receiver-ontology}

\outlineblock{Content}{
Document original-training, augmentation-bank, and evaluation-holdout source
sets; training, seen-anchor, and fresh receivers; semantic-family separation;
and the physical-compatibility mask. Report counts and validation hashes.}

\subsection{Freezing the Seven-Mechanism Scope}
\label{app:scope-freeze}

\outlineblock{Content}{
Document the pre-treatment capability study, exclusion of airflow, bounded
prompt-refresh smoke, decision timing, and the rule that capability evidence
cannot be reused as independent confirmation.}
