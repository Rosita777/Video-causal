\section{Blind Full-Video Evaluation and Statistical Protocol}
\label{app:evaluation-metrics}

Evaluation converts each anonymous 49-frame output into four atomic judgments
before aggregation; method identity is not an input to VLM or human scoring.
The frozen inventory contains 2,352 main-study videos and 96 Water/Fracture
identification additions.  This appendix specifies what an evaluator sees,
how the anonymous package is separated from its keys, how VLM prelabels are
audited by humans, and how the resulting canonical atoms become registered
claims.

\subsection{Anonymous Package and Key Separation}
\label{app:anonymous-package}

Every physical output is bound to the frozen generation ledger and assigned a
pseudorandom review identifier derived from a secret-keyed digest of its ledger
record.  A public assignment exposes the mechanism-specific semantic context
needed for judgment---prompt, source, receiver, trigger, footprint, requested
counterfactual state, and, where applicable, specificity fields---but removes
the stream, method, backbone, checkpoint, adapter, case identifier, generation
seed, and source path.  Mechanism identity is therefore rubric context rather
than a hidden variable; treatment identity is hidden.  Each assignment shows
one anonymous video, never a side-by-side method comparison, and the
identification outputs are interleaved with the main inventory.

Passes A and B use the same 2,448 anonymous identifiers and byte-identical
composites, but their item orders have independent commitments.  Public
commitments bind the generation ledger, blind key, five-file evaluation-code
registry, and the three private mapping tiers in
Table~\ref{tab:key-separation}.  The formal VLM launcher validates only the
public tree and records zero private files opened by that process.

\subsection{Protocol Deviation and Remaining Blinding}
\label{app:evaluation-deviations}

The registered and observed evaluation sequences differ at the opening of the
full method key.  Both VLM passes were completed from the public-only package:
neither pass received a method mapping or the other pass's answer.  Separately,
a read-only audit opened the full key before human
canonicalization and before the exploratory A/B-mean aggregation rule was
materialized.  The tables produced by that later rule are explicitly
post-unblinding, non-canonical writing previews and cannot enter final results
or claim gates.

The frozen human queue contains neither method identities nor VLM scores.
Reviewers who receive isolated copies of its public directory can therefore
remain process-locally answer-key blind.  This remaining blinding does not
restore a globally pre-unblinding analysis: investigator-level access to the
full key is irreversible, and final human review cannot retroactively remove
that deviation.

The five evaluation implementations bound by the original code registry
remain byte-identical.  A frozen additive v2 amendment now binds that registry
and the deviation declaration.  Its wrappers change only manifest schema,
provenance wording, and orchestration: every rubric, sampling, substitution,
expansion, eligibility, estimability, bootstrap, multiplicity, margin, and
claim rule remains the registered v1 computation.  The human-review process
must still establish its own answer-key blindness through isolated deliveries
and explicit process attestations.

The registered key sequence is anonymous canonical atoms, then tier-1
Original eligibility and shared subsets, and only then tier-2 deanonymization
and metrics.  The amendment changes the provenance description of that
sequence, not its computations.
The amendment self-digest begins \texttt{f65652b23bc8}.  The out-of-tree
pre-metric manifest begins \texttt{b3892f5d6b59} and binds clean implementation
commit \texttt{b0fffa4fc8d9} (tree \texttt{4edcade573bf}), the sanitized
review instructions, the tested release environment, the final table
exporter, and the additive wrappers.  Its prefix artifact DAG passes the
registered pre-metric verifier.  This closes the provenance-before-labeling
gate without changing any registry-bound v1 byte; final human-canonical
reporting remains pending.

\subsection{Full-49-Frame Temporal Representation}
\label{app:temporal-panels}

A sparse sample of frames can miss a short trigger, confuse a pre-existing
mark with a causal footprint, or hide a late source reappearance.  Each review
input therefore contains all 49 indexed frames in the five overlapping
windows listed in Table~\ref{tab:temporal-panels}.  Every window has 13 frame
tiles, so the overlap preserves context at panel boundaries.

The deterministic renderer center-crops without letterboxing, resizes each
tile to \(112\times64\) with Lanczos resampling, labels its frame index, and
packs the panels into one \(1456\times520\) JPEG.  The fixed quality is 82 and
the maximum payload is 3 MiB.  A/B receive byte-identical composites.  Both
the VLM prompt and the human protocol require judgments from visible frames;
the textual prompt describes the requested scene but never proves that an
object, trigger, or footprint appears.

\subsection{Two Independently Ordered VLM Passes}
\label{app:vlm-review}

The formal prelabeler is the same judge in two independently ordered passes,
not two different models.  Both passes use \texttt{gpt-5.6-luna} with low
reasoning effort, temperature 1, a 1,600-token response budget, and response
storage disabled.  Each covers all 2,448 assignments.  The judge is instructed
to label one video independently, avoid inferring the method, avoid choosing a
winner, and treat a partial score as valid scientific evidence rather than a
failure to return a binary answer.

For a registered same-footprint alternative-cause case, a visible footprint
is not penalized when the allowed alternative visibly causes it and the
protected object remains noncausal.  For every field, the strict response
schema requires one integer score, an independent confidence in \([0,1]\),
and one to eight nonempty observations tied to distinct frame indices in
\(\{0,\ldots,48\}\); extra fields are rejected.  A refusal, timeout, model
mismatch, incomplete response, API failure, or schema error is infrastructure
failure rather than a scientific score.  Only the affected identifier may be
retried, in a fresh run root, and no zero-value scientific fallback is
permitted.  Both completed merge manifests contain 2,448 schema-valid rows
and zero fallbacks.

\subsection{Human Calibration, Audit, and Adjudication}
\label{app:human-audit}

The two VLM passes produce 9,792 unique video-field atoms, each scored twice.
The audit seed is 8,202,600.  A deterministic stratified sample selects
\(\lceil0.10n\rceil\) atoms within each mechanism, opaque stream stratum, and
field for calibration.  The queue also includes every A/B atomic disagreement,
every atom scored partial by either pass, every atom whose confidence in
either pass is below 0.75, and all atoms from an output marked unusable by
either pass.  An output is unusable if either pass supplies an explicit
unusable reason or assigns zero to receiver preservation or video quality.
A separate stratified \(\lceil0.10n\rceil\) sample is drawn from usable,
non-partial A/B agreements for which both confidences are at least 0.75.
These rules overlap;
the current frozen union contains 3,633 atoms spanning 2,009 anonymous videos.

Two humans must score this same frozen set independently from isolated copies
of the public package.  They use the definitions in
Table~\ref{tab:atomic-rubric}, cannot inspect private keys or each other's
labels, and may alter only their designated score and note fields.  Agreement
becomes the resolved human score; every disagreement requires a third
adjudicator, and an adjudicator entry is forbidden when the first two scores
agree.

After the initial process receipt is frozen, the registered expansion
decision always creates a final audit child.  Each reviewer receives a
separate projection that retains only that reviewer's own prior columns,
blanks peer and adjudicator columns, and leaves any added atoms blank.  A
final process receipt is required even when the decision adds no atoms; only
this receipt can authorize canonical freeze.

Audit expansion is determined separately for each mechanism-by-field stratum.
Among sampled high-confidence A/B agreements, an error occurs when the
resolved human score differs from the common VLM score.  A rate strictly above
5\% expands that stratum to all remaining atoms, and a triggered expansion is
irreversible.  In the final anonymous canonical table, every audited atom uses
human consensus or adjudication.  An unaudited atom is retained only when A
and B agree on a non-partial score, both confidences meet the threshold, and
the output is usable.

\outlineblock{Final human-canonical reporting}{
Human labeling has not begun.  After adjudication and any expansion, report
VLM--VLM disagreement; pass-specific calibration and targeted-audit macro-F1
and quadratic-weighted kappa against resolved human labels; human atom/video
coverage; human disagreement; expanded strata and atoms; and the final
canonical artifact hashes.}

\subsection{Eligibility, CES, and Specificity Utility}
\label{app:metric-definitions}

Original eligibility is a capability condition on the corresponding unedited
backbone, not evidence of erasure.  Using the notation of
Sec.~\ref{subsec:desiderata},
\begin{align}
  E_b(u)
  &=
  \mathbb{1}\!\left[
    S_{\operatorname{Orig}(b)}(u)=2,\,
    F_{\operatorname{Orig}(b)}(u)\geq1,\,
    R_{\operatorname{Orig}(b)}(u)\geq1,\,
    Q_{\operatorname{Orig}(b)}(u)\geq1
  \right], \\
  U_k(u)
  &=
  \mathbb{1}\!\left[R_k(u)\geq1,\;Q_k(u)\geq1\right], \\
  \ces_k(u)
  &=
  E_b(u)U_k(u)
  \frac{(2-S_k(u))+(2-F_k(u))}{4}.
  \label{eq:appendix-ces}
\end{align}
An Original-ineligible case and an unusable method output remain in the fixed
denominator but contribute zero.  On an eligible, usable case with both source
and footprint scored partial, \(\ces=0.5\); the metric therefore represents
graded progress without requiring complete disappearance.  It is an absolute
gated output score.  Efficacy is tested by a registered paired contrast, not
by a positive score on one output.

For specificity,
\begin{align}
  U_k^{\mathrm{sp}}(u)
  &=
  \mathbb{1}\!\left[R_k(u)\geq1,\;Q_k(u)\geq1\right], \\
  \su_k(u)
  &=
  U_k^{\mathrm{sp}}(u)
  \frac{P_k(u)+N_k(u)}{4}.
  \label{eq:appendix-su}
\end{align}
Both \(\ces\) and \(\su\) lie in \([0,1]\), with larger values better.
Receiver preservation, video quality, usability, and Original capability are
reported separately.  Cases are first averaged within each mechanism; the
seven mechanism means then receive equal weight \(1/7\).  No mechanism or
Original-ineligible case is dropped after outputs are observed.

\subsection{Inference, Multiplicity, and Claim Gates}
\label{app:statistical-protocol}

Each semantic case has one fixed generation seed, so the inferential unit is
the case rather than the seed.  For the primary same-backbone comparison,
\begin{equation}
  C_{\mathrm{Matched}}(u)
  =
  \ces_{\mathrm{SRCD}}(u)-\ces_{\mathrm{Matched}}(u).
  \label{eq:matched-contrast}
\end{equation}
For external CogVideoX method \(q\), the registered shared-capability contrast
is
\begin{equation}
  C_q(u)
  =
  \bigl(\ces_{\mathrm{SRCD}}(u)-\ces_{\mathrm{WanOrig}}(u)\bigr)
  -
  \bigl(\ces_q(u)-\ces_{\mathrm{CogOrig}}(u)\bigr).
  \label{eq:external-contrast}
\end{equation}
Equation~\ref{eq:external-contrast} is evaluated only where both Originals are
eligible.  A method-by-mechanism cell with fewer than 12 of 24 shared cases is
not estimable.  An external macro is formed only if all seven mechanism cells
are estimable; otherwise it is reported as NE rather than zero or
\(p=1\).  The final exporter must render the complete inference row as NE and
must not expose an internal sentinel p-value.

For headline, preservation, implicit-footprint, and held-out-source analyses,
paired cases are resampled with replacement within each mechanism and the
seven resampled mechanism means are averaged equally.  The identification
analysis instead resamples its 12 causal cases separately within Water and
Fracture and averages those two mechanism means equally; its specificity
non-inferiority analysis analogously resamples 12 specificity cases inside
each of those mechanisms.  The registered
analysis uses 10,000 PCG64 replicates with seed 8,202,601 for the former
analyses and seed 8,202,602 for identification.  The reported interval is the
unadjusted 2.5--97.5 percentile interval.  A superiority p-value is
\((1+\#\{C^\ast\leq0\})/10001\).  Holm's step-down procedure at family-wise
\(\alpha=0.05\) covers the five headline contrasts against Matched, Negative
Prompt, VideoEraser, T2VUnlearning-adapted, and SAFREE-CogVideoX.  A registered win
requires estimability, a positive point estimate, an interval lower bound
above zero, and a Holm-adjusted one-sided p-value below 0.05.  The phrase
\emph{outperforms all baselines} requires all five wins.

strictly above its registered margin.  A superiority p-value against zero is
reported by the metric builder but is not a non-inferiority p-value.  The full
role-conditioned claim additionally requires the SRCD--Matched headline win
and positive seven-mechanism macro estimates on implicit-footprint and
held-out-source cases.  For each identification control, its 12 Water and 12
Fracture causal cases must yield a positive two-mechanism mean, an interval
lower bound above zero, and a Holm-adjusted p-value below 0.05 in the
two-control family.  Its equal-weight mean over the six implicit cases in each
mechanism must be positive, and its 12 specificity cases per mechanism must
place the Specificity Utility interval lower bound above \(-0.10\).  All four
primary margins in Table~\ref{tab:noninferiority-margins} must also pass.
Complete absence, strict success, and the six registered causal--specificity
pairs per mechanism (42 total) remain secondary descriptive outcomes rather
than promotion gates.
