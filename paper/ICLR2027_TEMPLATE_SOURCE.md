The optional official math_commands.tex is not loaded because this project
already has a small notation layer in preamble/macros.tex. The official sample
paper and sample bibliography are retained in the downloaded archive rather
than copied into the manuscript source.

Submission contract checked from the official guide:

- initial main text: at most 9 pages;
- discussion/rebuttal and camera-ready main text: at most 10 pages;
- references: unlimited and excluded from the main-text limit;
- appendices: unlimited after references, either in the same PDF or separate;
- review: double blind across the main paper and supplementary material;
- AI Use Statement: required and excluded from the page limit;
- Reproducibility Statement: recommended and excluded from the page limit;
- Ethics Statement: recommended when applicable and excluded from the page
  limit, with a one-page maximum.

Do not enable camera-ready mode in submission.tex. The official camera-ready
switch will be introduced only after acceptance and after real author metadata
is added.
