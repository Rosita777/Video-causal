\section{Causal-Role Erasure}
\label{sec:problem-formulation}

\outlineblock{Section purpose}{
Define the intervention unit before presenting the implementation. A reader
should finish this section knowing what must disappear, what must remain, and
why ordinary object-erasure success criteria are insufficient.}

\subsection{Source, Receiver, and Causal Footprint}
\label{subsec:event-anatomy}

\outlineblock{Definitions}{
Represent a physical event with a causal source, receiver, trigger,
downstream footprint, and scene context. Define each term operationally and
instantiate it for water impact, rigid collision, brittle fracture, powder
impact, elastic deformation, material release, and surface trace. Place the
complete ontology in \appref{app:task-ontology}.}

\subsection{The Role-Conditioned Source-Free World}
\label{subsec:no-source-world}

\outlineblock{Counterfactual target}{
Define the desired distribution in which the causal source, trigger, and
source-dependent footprint are absent while the receiver and surrounding
scene remain valid and dynamic. Emphasize that this is a distributional
counterfactual target, not the pixel-level counterfactual of one factual
video.}

\subsection{Three Desiderata}
\label{subsec:desiderata}

\outlineblock{Behavioral contract}{
The edited model must: (i) reduce source and footprint evidence for causal
prompts; (ii) preserve the same nouns and legitimate alternative footprints
in noncausal prompts; and (iii) retain the receiver, temporal quality, and
overall usability. Introduce \ces\ and \su\ conceptually and defer their
exact scoring and inference to \S\ref{subsec:experimental-setup} and
\appref{app:evaluation-metrics}.}

\claimboundary{The source role is supplied by the structured data. The task is
role-conditioned intervention learning, not unsupervised causal discovery.}
