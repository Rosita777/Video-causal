\section{Scope and Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our scope is deliberately narrower than universal concept erasure.
\methodshort{} is instantiated as seven mechanism-specific adapters on Wan 2.1
T2V 1.3B, not one seven-mechanism adapter.  The Wan block also contains seven
Matched adapters and four Water/Fracture lexical-control adapters; a separate
CogVideoX-2B block evaluates Original and four benchmark interfaces.  Each
training run uses one committed seed, and each semantic case uses one
generation seed shared across compatible streams.  We therefore do not
estimate sensitivity to random initialization or cross-backbone robustness of
\methodshort{}, which is evaluated only on Wan.  The scope is further limited
to locked-camera events within the registered prompt distribution.  Held-out
sources test transfer within this ontology, not open-world causal
generalization.

The supervision target is also limited.  Each source-free video is an
independently generated and screened proxy for a desired distribution, not the
unique individual counterfactual paired with one factual realization.  Event
roles are provided by the data construction rather than discovered by the
model.  Even positive outcomes under source-slot randomization and the
registered controls can therefore support at most a role-conditioned
behavioral interpretation, not an internal causal representation.

Evaluation inherits both capability and judgment constraints.  Causal credit
is gated by whether the same-backbone Original produces an eligible event;
this prevents erasure credit for a missing base capability but narrows the
estimand.  External comparisons normalize to different Originals and remain
cross-backbone benchmark comparisons rather than pure ablations.  The atomic
(0/1/2) rubric records partial visibility, so improvement need not mean
complete disappearance.  Finally, a separate read-only audit opened the full
method key before human canonicalization; global pre-unblinding cannot be
claimed.  The reviewer-delivery design still keeps each human process-locally
answer-key blind, while post-unblinding VLM previews remain excluded from final
scientific claims.
