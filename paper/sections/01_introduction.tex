\outlineblock{Paragraph 1 - the overlooked failure}{
Open with the mismatch between object-centric erasure and physical events.
An edited video can reduce the named source while retaining the splash,
fracture, deformation, release, or trace that it caused. Establish why this
residual footprint remains evidence of the erased event.}

\outlineblock{Paragraph 2 - role, not noun}{
Explain why unconditional noun removal is also wrong. The same entity may be a
causal source, a receiver, or a bystander. A valid method must intervene on its
role in an event rather than suppress every use of its name or appearance.}

\outlineblock{Paragraph 3 - technical obstacles}{
Introduce the three hard cases that determine the design: the footprint may be
implicit in the prompt, source identities must generalize beyond training, and
the desired no-source video is distributional rather than pixel-aligned with a
single factual realization.}

\outlineblock{Paragraph 4 - method idea}{
Introduce \methodname\ as one mechanism, not a component list: vary the
identity occupying the causal-source slot while holding the corresponding
dynamic no-source target fixed, then distill toward that target under a
preservation constraint. Point to \S\ref{sec:method}.}

\outlineblock{Paragraph 5 - evidence and boundary}{

the two-mechanism Water/Fracture identification study, implicit-footprint and
held-out-source subsets, and specificity guardrails. State the supported
