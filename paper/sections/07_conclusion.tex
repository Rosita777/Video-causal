\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Causal-role erasure asks a T2V generator to suppress an entity as the
registered source of an event, reduce its dependent footprint, and preserve
the same noun in registered noncausal uses.  \methodshort{} addresses this
contract by varying source identity while holding a dynamic source-free target
and the remaining training recipe fixed.  The Matched and lexical controls,
together with held-out-source, implicit-footprint, and specificity evaluations,
are designed to distinguish role-conditioned behavior from noun suppression
without treating any such behavior as evidence of an internal causal
representation.  This source--footprint--specificity decomposition turns
object disappearance into a falsifiable model-editing contract.
