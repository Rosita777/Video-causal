\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{subsec:experimental-setup}

\paragraph{Mechanisms and cases.}
We evaluate seven equal-status mechanisms: water impact, rigid collision,
brittle fracture, powder impact, elastic deformation, material release, and
surface trace. Each contributes 24 causal and 18 specificity cases. The causal
set balances three source--receiver generalization groups and crosses direct
or natural wording with explicit or implicit footprint descriptions. The
specificity set crosses three source memberships, two wording styles, and
three noncausal subtypes: same-noun noncausal use, role swap or near-causal
use, and the
same footprint produced by an alternative cause. This gives 294 semantic
cases. Eight main streams produce 2,352 videos; Generic Paraphrase and
Bystander Token on a frozen 48-case Water/Fracture subset add 96, for 2,448
formal outputs.

\paragraph{Models and baselines.}
The eight main streams are separated by backbone. The Wan block contains Wan Original,
\matchedcontrol, and \methodshort, while the CogVideoX block contains
CogVideoX Original, \negativeprompt, \videoeraser, \tvtwounlearning, and
\safree. Methods within each block receive the same positive case prompt and
seed. The
Wan \methodshort{}--Matched contrast is the single-factor comparison. Raw
scores across backbone blocks are descriptive; registered external contrasts
instead normalize each method by its same-backbone Original and will be
restricted to cases for which both Originals satisfy the registered
eligibility criterion. These remain cross-backbone normalized benchmark
comparisons rather than pure method ablations.

\paragraph{Evaluation and statistics.}
Every output receives a randomized review identifier, and the public package
removes method, backbone, checkpoint, and source-path cues while interleaving
all streams. Each judgment covers all 49 indexed frames through five
overlapping temporal panels. Two independently ordered passes of the same registered
VLM judge assign the registered \(0/1/2\) atomic scores for source or
protected-object visibility, footprint visibility or noncausal-role
adherence, receiver preservation, and video quality. These outputs are
prelabels rather than final authority. Two process-locally answer-key-blind
humans will independently score the registered audit atoms, and a third will
adjudicate every disagreement. A mechanism-by-field stratum will expand to all
remaining atoms when the audited high-confidence A/B agreements disagree with
resolved human labels at a rate strictly above 5\%. Final paper statistics
will use the
resulting post-adjudication canonical table: audited atoms use human consensus
or adjudication, while an unaudited atom may retain only qualifying
high-confidence VLM agreement. As defined in
Sec.~\ref{subsec:desiderata}, \ces{} gives graded causal-erasure credit only
when the same-backbone Original is eligible and the output remains usable;
\su{} measures protected-object visibility and noncausal-role adherence on
usable specificity cases. We average cases within each mechanism and weight
the seven means equally. Inference uses 10,000 paired,
mechanism-stratified case-bootstrap replicates, unadjusted 2.5--97.5
percentile intervals, Holm correction over five headline p-values, and prespecified
non-inferiority margins. Full rubrics, audit rules, provenance deviation, and
claim gates appear in \appref{app:evaluation-metrics}.
