# Cross-object preservation test for the waterdrop adapter.

Then one red apple falls visibly from above, strikes the center of the water, partially submerges, and produces a clear splash followed by expanding circular ripples. The camera, lighting, shoreline, and background remain unchanged. | red apple | apple_water_pond
